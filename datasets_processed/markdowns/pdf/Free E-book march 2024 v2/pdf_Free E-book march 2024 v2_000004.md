<|en|><|problem|>You’ll also need to download the Blender fSpy add-on, version 1.0.3. Without this add-on you won’t be able to import the files made in the fSpy app into Blender. Download the ZIP-file, but don’t unzip it! This goes for most blender add-ons that you download from the web. In Blender, go to Preferences and install the add-on. Click on Install and select the downloaded ZIP file. And finally enable it. You may have to search for it in the add-ons list.<|final|>
