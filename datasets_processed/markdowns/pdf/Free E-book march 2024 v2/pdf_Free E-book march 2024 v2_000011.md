Then scale it along the X-axis and move it to make it match the width of the gap. Then extrude along the Y-axis with E and Y. Move it slightly so there are no Faces overlapping in the exact same location (that would cause issues later). A quick check of the Face Orientation, shows that the Faces of this geometry are Flipped (shown in red). Pressing Shift+N recalculates the normals so they’re no longer flipped. I’m planning to do a boolean cut, and flipped Faces can cause trouble. Now with the extruded Faces selected, go to Face > Intersect Boolean. This cuts a nice chunk out of our building.<|final|>
