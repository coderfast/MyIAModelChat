The Schengen Agreement was signed on June 14, 1985, in the Luxembourgish town of Schengen, by five countries of the European Community: Belgium, France, Germany, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands. This historic agreement marked the first step toward the abolition of controls at internal borders between the signatory states and the creation of a free movement area for persons. The name Schengen comes from the town where the agreement was signed, located at the confluence of the borders of Luxembourg, France, and Germany.
